Ayah Echo Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026 · Nabil Pervez Consulting (NPC)

Ayah Echo is built to be private by design: no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains exactly what the app does with information.

What stays on your device

  • Your listening progress, history and statistics
  • Your settings (reciter, repeat count, etc.)

This data is stored in your browser/app storage on your device only. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

What we collect

Nothing. Ayah Echo has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Third-party services the app contacts

  • Netlify (hosts the app files)
  • AlQuran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud) for Quran text and translation
  • Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network) for recitation audio
  • Google Fonts for typefaces

These requests are needed to load the app and its content. Like any web request they include your IP address and device/browser type, which those providers may log under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
